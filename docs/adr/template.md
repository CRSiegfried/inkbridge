# ADR-NNNN: Title

Status: Proposed
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are in play — technical, business, licensing, whatever makes
this decision non-obvious? State the problem, not the answer.

## Decision

What we're doing, stated plainly in the active voice ("We will ...").

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we're explicitly giving up.
Include the downsides — an ADR with no costs listed didn't look hard enough.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. Keeps the next reader from
re-proposing something already ruled out for a reason they can't see.
